Use Case Stories

Goods Switzerland 🇨🇭 → Japan 🇯🇵

Kyoto Ramen from Switzerland

A user in Bern craving authentic ramen connects with a local in Kyoto. The local guides them through an online cooking class over Zoom, then curates and ships a package of specific local ingredients back to Switzerland.

Special Hawaii 🇺🇸 → Greece 🇬🇷

Storm Check in Greece

A homeowner living in Honolulu worries about their property in Athens after a severe storm is reported. They request a local to visit the address, take detailed photos and a short video to confirm the house weathered the storm safely.

Special France 🇫🇷 → USA 🇺🇸

Sneaker Drop in NYC

A passionate collector in Paris desperately wants a limited edition sneaker release. They pay a local in Manhattan to queue early in the morning outside the SoHo store and purchase the exact size on their behalf for later shipping.
